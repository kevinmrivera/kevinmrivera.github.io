Infrastructure domains

A quick view of the areas I typically own end-to-end, from design through implementation and support.

Cloud & Compute

Azure VMs Cost

VM and workload planning, segmentation, secure admin patterns, monitoring, and right-sizing to balance performance with cost.

  • Workload inventory + dependency mapping
  • Secure access patterns (jump/bastion-style)
  • Backup + restore validation and RPO/RTO alignment

Identity & Access

Entra ID SSO MFA

Hybrid identity foundations, SSO integrations, conditional access patterns, and access governance that reduces friction while improving control.

  • Hybrid identity alignment (directory + cloud)
  • Least privilege and role-based admin models
  • Access reviews and sign-in log driven tuning

Networking

VLANs VPN Firewall

Segmented networks, secure remote connectivity, and practical troubleshooting across routers, switches, Wi-Fi, and firewall policy.

  • VLAN segmentation + guest/corp isolation
  • Site-to-site and remote user VPN patterns
  • DNS, DHCP, routing, and layered troubleshooting

Endpoint Management

Intune Windows macOS

Standardized provisioning, configuration baselines, encryption posture, compliance reporting, and practical support at scale.

  • Policy baselines (encryption, updates, hardening)
  • Application deployment + lifecycle management
  • Compliance + device health reporting

Collaboration Platforms

M365 Google Interop

Tenant administration, mailbox and data migrations, and cross-platform interoperability to improve usability without weakening security.

  • Email migrations and staged cutovers
  • Calendar free/busy interoperability
  • Governance: sharing controls + audit readiness

Resilience & Operations

DR Runbooks Automation

Operational discipline: documentation, standard workflows, incident response coordination, and automation that reduces repeat work.

  • Backup strategy + recovery testing
  • Change management + rollout comms
  • Repeatable runbooks and checklists
